# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Bugbase to be an open, respectful, and technically serious community for everyone who participates.

Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to act professionally, communicate clearly, and treat each other with respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in technical and non-technical discussions
- Assume good intent, but stay rigorous in review and debate
- Accept feedback and course-correct when needed
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior, not people
- Help keep the project welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling, intimidation, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission
- Abusive language or conduct in issues, pull requests, chats, or other project spaces

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to `conduct@monodox.com`.
